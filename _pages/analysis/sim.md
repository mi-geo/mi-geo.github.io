## Simulated and computational models

{% include figure image_path="/assets/analysis/forest_fire.gif" 
   alt="Geosimulation example" 
   caption="Dynamic geosimulation: spatial patterns evolving over time." 
   width="100%" %}

Geosimulation models help us understand how **local rules and interactions** produce **large-scale spatial patterns**.  
The forest-fire model above is a classic **cellular automaton**, showing how ignition, spread, fuel availability, and spatial configuration shape system-wide outcomes.

I use **R, Python, and GIS tools** to build and visualize:

- **Cellular automata and agent-based models** (e.g., fire spread, segregation, diffusion)
- **Counterfactual spatial simulations** to explore alternative spatial arrangements  
- **Animated visualizations** of model evolution across space and time  

These simulations provide a flexible framework for studying **spatial processes**, testing **what-if scenarios**, and generating **comparative insights** across different regions or conditions.
